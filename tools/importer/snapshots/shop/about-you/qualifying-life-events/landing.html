Special Enrollment Periods

A Special Enrollment Period (SEP) is defined as a period during which you and your family have a right to sign up for new or make changes to existing health insurance coverage. SEP qualifying life events include, but are not limited to, certain permanent moves, and changes in your family size (such as if you marry, birth or adopt a child), or loss of coverage.

This SEP form CANNOT be used to make changes to coverage purchased from Pennie, Pennsylvania's official health insurance marketplace, or to purchase coverage from Pennie. To make such changes or purchases, you must contact the Pennie directly at www.pennie.com or 1-844-844-8040.

Making any change to your current Highmark policy may result in change to your current premium; by submitting the SEP or Change Form you are agreeing to accept those changes. The deductible amount and out-of-pocket maximum for your new individual coverage will reset on January 1st.

The list of qualifying events is subject to change. If you do not see the qualifying event that describes your situation, need an enrollment application, or have questions, please contact us at 1-855-873-4106. Failure to provide all information required may delay your coverage effective date or cause you to be denied coverage. The effective date of coverage will be determined by the receipt date of completed information as specified. The coverage effective date cannot be prior to the occurrence of the event. Applications received outside of the required SEP time frame for the specified qualifying life event will be denied.  For more information on SEP submission deadlines, please visit www.pennie.com
