      CHIP - Highmark Healthy Kids Eligibility and Costs

      Am I eligible?

      Most children receive CHIP - Highmark Healthy Kids for free. For children who live in a household with a higher income, out-of-pocket costs like monthly premiums and copays may apply.

      Your children are eligible if they're:

      • under 19 years of age.
      • A U.S. citizen, U.S. national, or a qualified legal resident.
      • A resident of Pennsylvania.
      • Uninsured and not able to receive medical assistance.
      Please call the Statewide Customer Service Center at 1-877-395-8930. For Philadelphia: 215-560-7226. for eligibility questions.

      Income guidelines

      Your household income will determine your monthly premium and copays.
