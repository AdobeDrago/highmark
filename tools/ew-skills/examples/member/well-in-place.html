Well in Place

Well in Place is a service that Highmark offers free of charge to members who would benefit from specialized care coordination and/or supportive care while maintaining their independence in the community.

What to expect from the Well in Place team

An interdisciplinary care team led by a specialist physician and including an Advanced Practice Provider (APP; nurse practitioner or physician assistant), Registered Nurse (RN), Licensed Social Worker (LSW) and Care Coordinator. Our team of clinicians are ready to help support you with:

  • Working towards your goals: We will work with you and your family to help ensure your care plan aligns with your health care wishes.
  • Coordinating your care: Our team works closely with your doctors and health care providers. We can help identify additional resources that may be beneficial to you.
  • Managing your symptoms: Our team helps you manage symptoms such as pain, shortness of breath, fatigue, constipation, nausea, loss of appetite, difficulty sleeping, and depression.
  • Educating you and your loved ones: We will make sure you, and your family, understand your illness, care plan, and treatments. We want you to feel confident in your health care decision-making and your future plans.

This program is available to all Highmark members who meet clinical criteria. Give us a call today to learn more about how we can support you.

Well in Place provides care in Pennsylvania and Delaware. If you do not live in either of those states, our Case Management team can support you. Learn more about Case Management services.
